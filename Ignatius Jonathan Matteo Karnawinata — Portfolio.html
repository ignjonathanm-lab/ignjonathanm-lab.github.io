Research Project

Quantitative Research · Team of 6 · BINUS University

The Influence of Social Media Usage and Digital Literacy on Students' Understanding of Drug Abuse

This study examines how students' social media usage and digital literacy relate to their understanding of the dangers of drug abuse. We surveyed students with a Likert-scale questionnaire and analyzed the data with PLS-SEM.

My role: I ran the data analysis in SmartPLS, evaluated the measurement and structural models, and interpreted the findings.

127Respondents (purposive sampling)
PLS-SEMAnalyzed with SmartPLS
0.712–0.886Outer loadings (all > 0.70)
< 0.90HTMT, discriminant validity met

H1 · Social Media Usage → Understanding

β = 0.584 · t = 6.866 · p < 0.001 — Supported

H2 · Digital Literacy → Understanding

β = −0.044 · t = 0.454 · p = 0.650 — Not supported

Key takeaway: students' understanding of drug abuse was shaped more by what they encounter on social media than by their ability to evaluate digital information. This suggests drug-prevention campaigns should combine targeted social media outreach with digital literacy education.
